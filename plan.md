# ProForce Finder Website Build Plan

## Goal

Build a professional single-page website for **ProForce Finder**, a construction staffing and talent scouting service.

The website should feel:
- Professional
- Trustworthy
- International
- Construction-industry focused
- Clean, modern, and serious
- Not too corporate or generic

The site should present ProForce Finder as a reliable operation that connects international construction companies with vetted, skilled workers.

---

## Page Structure

Create a single-page website with 5 main sections:

1. Hero
2. About
3. Services
4. Why Choose Us
5. Contact

Include a navigation menu that scrolls/slides to each section.

---

# 1. Hero Section

## Purpose

Immediately explain what ProForce Finder does and create trust.

## Content

### Headline

ProForce Finder: Precision Staffing for Global Construction

### Subheadline

Connecting international industry leaders with high-skilled, vetted construction professionals.

## Suggested CTA Buttons

Primary button:

Request Talent

Secondary button:

Learn More

## Design Direction

Use a strong construction/business visual style.

Suggested elements:
- Construction site image or subtle background
- Workers / cranes / steel / buildings
- Dark navy, steel gray, white, and safety orange accents
- Clean layout with strong typography
- Hero can use a split layout: text on one side, image/visual on the other

---

# 2. About Section

## Purpose

Explain who ProForce Finder is without using the word "company" too much.

## Content

ProForce Finder is a specialized talent scouting operation dedicated to the construction sector.

We focus on identifying and preparing professional teams for large-scale international projects.

Our goal is to ensure every placement is technically skilled, culturally prepared, and logistically ready for success abroad.

## Design Direction

Keep this section simple and confidence-building.

Suggested layout:
- Short text blocks
- Maybe 3 small cards:
    - Skilled Professionals
    - International Projects
    - Ready for Deployment

---

# 3. Our Services Section

## Purpose

Show the practical services ProForce Finder provides.

## Services

### Elite Talent Sourcing

Finding experienced workers tailored to your specific needs.

### Professional Skill Assessment

Ensuring the highest standards of workmanship.

### Bureaucratic Pre-Screening

Evaluating legal feasibility for international deployment.

### Language & Cultural Orientation

Providing basic training to ensure a smooth transition.

## Design Direction

Use cards or badges for each service.

Each card should include:
- Icon
- Service title
- Short description

Suggested icons:
- Search / target icon
- Checklist / certification icon
- Document / legal icon
- Globe / language icon

---

# 4. Why Choose Us Section

## Purpose

Explain why clients should trust ProForce Finder instead of generic recruiters.

## Content

### Modern Industry Experience

We exclusively source professionals with proven experience in modern construction environments and advanced building standards.

### Tailor-Made Solutions

Delivering the exact professional match for your project's requirements.

### Efficiency & Reliability

We handle the complexity of recruitment so you can stay focused on your deadlines.

## Design Direction

This section should feel like a trust-building section.

Suggested layout:
- 3 horizontal feature blocks
- Or 3 strong cards
- Add subtle statistics placeholders if useful, but do not invent real numbers

Example placeholder style:

Vetted Professionals  
International Readiness  
Project-Focused Matching

Do not use fake metrics like “500+ workers” unless provided by the client.

---

# 5. Contact Section

## Purpose

Make it easy for potential clients to contact ProForce Finder.

## Heading

Let’s Build Your Next Team

## Form Fields

- Name
- Business / Organization
- Email Address
- Message

## Button

Send Inquiry

## Design Direction

Use a simple contact form with a professional layout.

Suggested extras:
- Short intro sentence above form
- Contact section can have a darker background for contrast
- Add small trust line near the form:

Tell us what kind of team you need, and we’ll help you find the right professionals.

---

# Navigation

Create a navigation bar with links to:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Why Us
- Contact

The navigation should smoothly scroll to each section.

On mobile, use a hamburger menu or clean stacked navigation.

---

# Visual Style

## Brand Feeling

The website should feel like a professional construction staffing service, not a startup SaaS product.

## Suggested Colors

- Dark navy
- Steel gray
- White
- Light gray backgrounds
- Safety orange or construction yellow as accent

## Typography

Use clean, strong fonts.

Suggested style:
- Bold headlines
- Clear body text
- Good spacing
- Strong contrast

## Imagery

Use construction-related visuals, preferably:
- Construction workers
- Cranes
- Modern buildings
- Steel structures
- International logistics feel

Avoid images that look too cartoonish, childish, or overly clean.

---

# Responsiveness

The website must work well on:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile

Mobile priorities:
- Clear hero headline
- Easy CTA button
- Readable service cards
- Simple contact form

---

# SEO Basics

Use proper semantic HTML structure:

- One `h1` in hero
- Section headings as `h2`
- Service titles as `h3`

Suggested page title:

ProForce Finder | Construction Staffing for Global Projects

Suggested meta description:

ProForce Finder connects international construction leaders with skilled, vetted professionals prepared for global projects.

---

# Final Notes

Do not include Hebrew text on the page.

Keep the copy professional, direct, and polished.

Avoid fake numbers, fake testimonials, or unverifiable claims.

Focus on trust, skill, international readiness, and construction-sector expertise.
